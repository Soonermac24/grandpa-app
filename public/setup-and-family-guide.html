Setting up Papa’s screen on the Chromebook

Do this once, at Papa’s house. Takes about 5 minutes.

1. Open the screen in Chrome

  1. Plug in the Chromebook and connect it to Papa’s Wi-Fi.
  2. Open Chrome and type this address, then press Enter:
    https://grandpa-app-roan.vercel.app/display
  3. You should see a dark screen that says “Family Messages” and a green “Papa is home” badge.

2. Make it full screen & pin it

  1. Press the 🔳 full-screen key at the top of the Chromebook keyboard (or F11) so only the messages show — no toolbars.
  2. Pin it to the shelf as an app: Chrome menu (⋮) → More tools → Create shortcut… → check “Open as window” → Create. Now there’s a Papa App icon on the shelf he can tap.

3. Keep the screen awake

  1. Open Settings → Device → Power.
  2. Set “When idle” to Keep display on (while charging).
  3. Leave the Chromebook plugged in. The screen should stay on all day.
💡 Whenever this screen is open, family sees “Papa is home.” Closing it shows “Papa is away.” So just leave it running.

4. Show Papa the Guide

A short narrated video that teaches everything on this sheet.

  1. On the screen, tap “❔ How to use this” at the top — or go to https://grandpa-app-roan.vercel.app/guide
  2. Tap ▶ Start the guide. Use the “Skip to…” buttons on the right to jump to any part.

5. The QR code

  1. Print the QR sheet (/print-qr.html) and tape the “Talk to Papa” code to the back of the screen.
  2. Family scans it with their phone camera to send messages.

Papa App · For Family

How to talk to Papa

Papa can’t hear well — but he can read. Send him a message and it appears in big letters on his screen at home.

1. Point your phone camera at the QR code on the back of Papa’s screen. Tap the link.

2. Type your name the first time (so Papa knows who’s talking). Your phone remembers it.

3. Hold the big microphone button, say your message, then let go.

4. Your words pop up on Papa’s screen in a few seconds. 🎉

Great-grandkids — helping Papa in person

  • Make the words bigger: tap the small ⚙ gear (bottom-right) → tap A+. Tap a colored dot to change the color.
  • Caption a conversation: tap 🎧 Listen Mode at the top, then the big Listen button. Whatever people say shows up as words. Tap red Stop when done.
  • Watch the guide together: tap ❔ How to use this at the top of his screen.

Papa, when you’re out of the house

  • Open Papa App on your phone: https://grandpa-app-roan.vercel.app/read
  • Tap 🔔 Notify me once so your phone buzzes when family writes.
